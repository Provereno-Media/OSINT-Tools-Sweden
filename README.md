# OSINT-Tools-Sweden
<img src="https://flagcdn.com/w320/se.png" alt="Sweden_FLAG"/>
A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Sweden. Most of the resources and tools has versions in Swedish, many also in English. Thanks to the principle of public access (offentlighetsprincipen), Swedish people-search sites publish addresses, ages, relatives and income data, and vehicle and aircraft owners can be looked up. Since 1 July 2026 public electronic access to the register of beneficial owners (verkliga huvudmän) requires a legitimate interest, following the CJEU judgment of 22 November 2022. The Swedish Companies Registration Office (Bolagsverket) and several people-search sites block automated access, so those links were validated through independent sources and should be checked by hand. Enter names with Swedish letters (å, ä, ö).

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [Sveriges dataportal (Swedish Data Portal)](https://www.dataportal.se/datasets) — national catalogue of open datasets, APIs and specifications from Swedish public organisations; needs JavaScript. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Statistikdatabasen (Statistics Sweden — Statistical Database)](https://www.statistikdatabasen.scb.se/pxweb/sv/ssd/) — Statistics Sweden's database of official statistical tables, with downloads and an API. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Riksbanken — Statistik (Central Bank of Sweden — Statistics)](https://www.riksbank.se/sv/statistik/) — interest rates, exchange rates, financial-market and payments statistics. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Lagrummet](https://www.lagrummet.se/) — common website for Sweden's public legal information: laws, ordinances, preparatory works, agency regulations and case law. *Lang: SV.*
- [lagen.nu](https://lagen.nu/) — free service with all Swedish laws in force, EU acts, case law and preparatory works, linked and partly annotated. *Lang: SV.*
- [Högsta domstolen — Avgöranden (Supreme Court — Decisions)](https://www.domstol.se/hogsta-domstolen/avgoranden/) — precedents of the Supreme Court, leave-to-appeal decisions and pending preliminary references to the CJEU. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Post- och Inrikes Tidningar (Official Gazette)](https://poit.bolagsverket.se/) — Bolagsverket's official gazette: bankruptcies, liquidations, company registrations and changes, summonses and estate notices; needs JavaScript. *Lang: SV.*
- [Finansinspektionen — Insynsregistret (FSA — Insider Register)](https://www.fi.se/sv/vara-register/insynsregistret/) — transactions in listed companies by persons discharging managerial responsibilities and their closely associated persons. *Lang: SV, EN.*

## Legal Entities
- [Bolagsverket — Sök företagsinformation (Companies Registration Office — Company Search)](https://foretagsinfo.bolagsverket.se/) — official company search by organisation number or name: basic data such as name, address, registered office and officers are free without registration; copies of documents are paid. Blocks automated access. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Allabolag.se](https://www.allabolag.se/) — key figures, income statements, official positions and addresses for all companies filing annual reports with Bolagsverket; network search of links between people and companies. *Lang: SV.*
- [Finansinspektionen — Företagsregistret (FSA — Register of Companies)](https://www.fi.se/sv/vara-register/foretagsregistret/) — companies licensed by or registered with the Financial Supervisory Authority. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Syna](https://upplysningar.syna.se/) — credit-information company: credit reports on any Swedish company (about 90 SEK each) and market data. Paid. *Lang: SV.*
- [North Data](https://www.northdata.com/) — North Data covers company data in Sweden as part of its XL package, which includes comprehensive information on companies, key persons, and financials. *Lang: EN, DE, FR.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [Min karta (Lantmäteriet)](https://minkarta.lantmateriet.se/) — Swedish mapping, cadastral and land registration authority's map: topographic and aerial maps, property boundaries and property designations. *Lang: SV.*
- [Historiska kartor och akter (Lantmäteriet Historical Maps)](https://historiskakartor.lantmateriet.se/) — Lantmäteriet's archive of historical maps and cadastral survey records. *Lang: SV.*
- [Hitta.se — Kartan](https://www.hitta.se/kartan) — map with search of addresses, persons and companies. *Lang: SV.*
- [Fornsök (Swedish National Heritage Board)](https://app.raa.se/open/fornsok/) — register and map of ancient monuments and archaeological sites. *Lang: SV.*
- [SGU Kartvisare (Geological Survey of Sweden Map Viewer)](https://apps.sgu.se/kartvisare/) — geological maps: bedrock, soil, groundwater, wells and mineral resources. *Lang: SV.*
- [Skyddad natur (Swedish EPA — Protected Nature)](https://skyddadnatur.naturvardsverket.se/) — map tool of national parks, nature reserves, Natura 2000 sites and other protected areas. *Lang: SV.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Transportstyrelsen — Fordonsuppgifter (Vehicle Information)](https://fordon-fu-regnr.transportstyrelsen.se/) — Swedish Transport Agency's search by registration number or personalised plate; no e-ID needed to search other people's vehicles. *Lang: SV.*
- [Transportstyrelsen — SMS-tjänsten Vem äger fordonet (Vehicle Owner by SMS)](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/fordon/fordons-agaruppgift/sok-fordons-och-agaruppgifter/sms-tjansten-vem-ager-fordonet/) — send a registration number by SMS to 71640 and get the owner's name and town; free apart from operator charges. *Lang: SV.*
- [Car.info](https://www.car.info/) — vehicle data by registration number: specifications, history, valuation and statistics for Sweden, Norway and Denmark. Freemium: parts are available only to logged-in users. *Lang: SV, EN, NO, DA.*

### Maritime
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — international database of ship and shipping-company safety information: IMO number, flag, owner, manager, inspections. Free registration required. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [Transportstyrelsen — Sök uppgifter om luftfartyg (Aircraft Register Search)](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/luftfart/luftfartyg-och-luftvardighet/sok-uppgifter-om-luftfartyg/) — search SE- registered aircraft by registration or by owner/operator: registration status, type, serial number, year, MTOW, airworthiness, first registration, owner and operator. *Lang: SV.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Hitta.se](https://www.hitta.se/) — free directory: search persons, companies and places by name, street, town or phone number; "who called" lookup. *Lang: SV.*
- [Ratsit](https://www.ratsit.se/) — person and company search covering about 8 million people: free basic information (address, age, relatives); income and capital from the latest tax assessment via paid "Lönekollen". Ratsit says the person looked up is not notified. Blocks automated access. *Lang: SV.*
- [Riksdagen — Ledamöter och partier (Parliament — Members and Parties)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/ledamoter-och-partier/) — profiles of MPs; registration of MPs' commitments and economic interests is mandatory under law 1996:810. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Valmyndigheten — Valresultat 2026 (Election Authority — Results)](https://data.val.se/val2026) — official results of the 2026 general elections by party and area; earlier elections at val.se. *Lang: SV.*
- [Riksarkivet — Sök i arkiven (National Archives Search)](https://sok.riksarkivet.se/) — search of archives from state administration, companies, associations and persons in the National Archival Database and the Digital Research Room. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [Upphandlingsmyndigheten — Statistiktjänsten (National Agency for Public Procurement — Statistics)](https://www.upphandlingsmyndigheten.se/statistik/) — statistics database and analyses of public purchasing and procurement. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Konkurrensverket — Offentlig upphandling (Competition Authority — Public Procurement)](https://www.konkurrensverket.se/upphandling/) — procurement supervisor: rules, thresholds, supervision decisions and tip-offs. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Avropa.se (National Procurement Services)](https://www.avropa.se/) — Kammarkollegiet's central purchasing body: state framework agreements, advertised procurements and suppliers. *Lang: SV.*
- [Mercell — Sök upphandlingar (Tender Search)](https://info.mercell.com/sv-se/) — e-tendering platform used by many Swedish contracting authorities; public search of advertised procurements, paid plans for suppliers. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU procurement-notice search; filter by country Sweden for above-threshold notices and contract awards. *Lang: EN, multi.*

## WHOIS
- [Internetstiftelsen — Sök domän (Swedish Internet Foundation — Domain Search)](https://internetstiftelsen.se/sok-doman/) — official .se and .nu registry search of available domains and domain holders. *Lang: SV, EN.*
- [Internetstiftelsen — Zone Data](https://internetstiftelsen.se/en/zone-data/) — the complete .se and .nu zone files, available by DNS zone transfer (`dig @zonedata.iis.se se AXFR`), updated every half hour under CC BY 4.0; useful for finding all domains on given name servers. *Lang: EN, SV.*
- [IANA — .se Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/se.html) — official delegation data for .se: registry operator, contacts and name servers. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Swedish networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
